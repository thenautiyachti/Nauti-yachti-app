# What he should actually make

Built from real Roblox video titles found in the wild, not from memory. I
cannot watch video, so what follows is read off **titles, descriptions and
formats** — the shape of what gets posted, not a judgement of how any
particular one was made.

---

## The one idea that matters

Earlier I found that **scripted moments outperform raw gameplay**, and flagged
it as a limit on the clip cutter: no amount of clever editing invents a reason
to watch. Scanning actual titles turned up what "scripted" means in practice in
this niche, and it is not writing sketches. It is **adding one rule**:

> `BLINDFOLDED ROBLOX OBBY CHALLENGE`
> `Roblox, But WE'RE BLIND, DEAF, AND MUTE!`
> `Guiding my **BLINDFOLDED** friend through an OBBY!`
> `Obby But You Are Blind`
> `Guess The Roblox Game BUT WE CAN'T SPEAK`
> `I PLAYED *COLOR BLIND* OBBY!`

That is the whole trick, and it is close to perfect for his situation:

- **Zero prep and zero skill.** The rule is decided in ten seconds before you
  start. There is nothing to write and nothing to act.
- **It needs friends, which he has.** Most of these formats are *better* with
  two or three people, because the comedy is the arguing.
- **It generates its own good moments.** The cutter looks for seconds that are
  loud *and* busy. A blindfolded friend walking off a ledge while everyone
  shouts is exactly that — so the constraint formats and the tool were built
  for each other by accident.
- **It works with no face and no camera.** The Roblox avatar is the character.
  Nobody has to be on screen.

If he only takes one thing from this file: **decide one rule before pressing
record.** That single habit is worth more than everything else here.

## Formats, best fit first

Each one is rated for *can he do this, on a tablet, with his friends, this
weekend*.

### 1. The constraint challenge ★ start here
One rule that makes an easy game hard. Blindfolded (the other player describes
where to go), no talking, no jumping, one finger only, can't let go of each
other, only one colour.

- **Title:** `[GAME] But We Can't [RULE]` · `BLINDFOLDED [GAME] Challenge` · `Guiding my BLINDFOLDED friend through [GAME]`
- **Thumbnail:** layout 1 (Reaction) — the rule in three words, arrow at the chaos
- **Effort:** none. **Works with friends:** essential.

### 2. Forced co-op games
Games built so two people *must* cooperate — Hold Hands Obby and its relatives.
The game supplies the conflict; nobody has to invent anything.

- **Title:** `We Had To [MECHANIC] The WHOLE Game` · `2 Player [GAME] With My Friend`
- **Thumbnail:** layout 2 (Versus) — both avatars, seam down the middle
- **Effort:** none. **Works with friends:** required, it's two-player.

### 3. First time playing
Genuinely his first go at a game, reacting honestly. Real titles: `First Time
Playing Grow a Garden Roblox`, `So I Played Grow a Garden on Roblox… And It's
Surprisingly Addicting!`

- **Title:** `First Time Playing [GAME] in Roblox` · `I Tried [GAME] For The First Time`
- **Thumbnail:** layout 1 (Reaction)
- **Effort:** none — but it is **once per game, ever.** Spend them on games that
  are blowing up, while they are blowing up.

### 4. Survived / endurance
Real titles: `I Survived 99 Nights in the Forest`. A number in the title, a
clear finish line, obvious stakes.

- **Title:** `I Survived [N] [UNIT] in [GAME]` · `[N] Hours of [GAME] With My Friends`
- **Thumbnail:** layout 3 (Challenge) — the number enormous. This is the layout
  that survives being shrunk the smallest.
- **Effort:** low, but it needs a real session.

### 5. Versus
Real title: `ADLEY vs MOM vs DAD vs NiCK vs ALLi`. Him against a friend, or a
sibling, or you.

- **Title:** `[HIM] vs [FRIEND] in [GAME]` · `NOOB vs PRO in Roblox [GAME]`
- **Thumbnail:** layout 2 (Versus)
- **Effort:** none. A parent losing badly is reliably the best version of this.

### 6. Milestone / first rare thing
Real title: `I Got My FIRST Mythic Plant Seed in Roblox Grow a Garden`. Only
works if it genuinely happened — which is the point, it is a reason to keep
recording ordinary sessions.

- **Title:** `I Got My FIRST [RARE THING] in Roblox [GAME]`
- **Thumbnail:** layout 3 (Challenge) or 4 (Series)
- **Effort:** none, but unplanned. Record everything; the cutter finds it later.

### 7. Noob to pro
Real title: `NOOB To PRO in Roblox Grow a Garden`. Naturally a series, which is
what layout 4 is for.

- **Title:** `NOOB to PRO in Roblox [GAME] — Part [N]`
- **Thumbnail:** layout 4 (Series) — the number badge in the *same spot* every
  episode
- **Effort:** medium. It only pays off if he keeps going.

## Not worth his time

- **Codes and update videos** (`ALL NEW CODES in Blox Fruits Update 24`). Huge
  search volume and a genuine format — but it is a race decided in minutes by
  adults with alerts set. A kid uploading after school has already lost.
- **Contrarian takes** (`GROW A GARDEN IS UNENJOYABLE!!! I HATE...`). This
  works for established creators. From a small kids' channel it is just a child
  being negative on the internet, and there is no upside worth it.
- **Anything needing a face cam or editing software.** He is on a tablet, and
  none of the formats above need either.

## Which games

As of now the ones with the audience are **Steal a Brainrot**, **Grow a
Garden**, **Brookhaven**, **Blox Fruits** and **99 Nights in the Forest** —
the last of which is repeatedly described as suitable for about ages 5+, which
makes it an easy one to say yes to.

But pick from what **he already plays and can talk over**. Enthusiasm is
audible and a kid cannot fake it, and a channel about a game he does not care
about dies in three weeks. Stay on one to three games.

## Title conventions, from the real ones

Every title above does the same four things:

1. **The game's name, spelled exactly.** This is how it gets found.
2. **The word "Roblox"**, usually — `Roblox Grow a Garden`, or `(Roblox)` on the end.
3. **One or two words in CAPS**, never the whole title: `INSANE`, `FIRST`, `MOST`, `BLINDFOLDED`.
4. **First person.** `I Got`, `We Had To`, `My First`. Not `Gameplay Episode 4`.

Asterisks around the key word (`*COLOR BLIND*`) is a real convention in this
niche if he likes it.

## A first month

Eight uploads, no new skills, no equipment.

| week | long video | Shorts from it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A constraint challenge with one friend | 3 |
| 2 | First time playing whatever is currently big | 3 |
| 3 | Him vs you, same game as week 1 | 3 |
| 4 | Survived-style session with both friends | 3 |

Record one session a week, run the cutter, post the long video and three Shorts
off the same recording, and cross-post those Shorts to TikTok and Reels. That
is roughly four posts a week across platforms from one afternoon of playing —
which is how the frequency numbers get reached without anybody doing four
afternoons of work.

Then look at which of the four got watched, and do more of that one. Four
honest attempts beats any amount of planning, including this file.

---

## Before any of it

The friends are in these videos by name and voice. **Ask their parents first**,
every time, and tell them it is going on a public YouTube channel. It is a
thirty-second conversation now and an unpleasant one later.

No full names, no school, no town, no street. The avatars can be as
recognisable as he likes; he cannot be.

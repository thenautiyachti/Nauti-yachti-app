- Capsizing, collision with other vessels, submerged objects, or the shoreline;

- Injury from watersports equipment (tubing, wakeboarding, skiing, swimming,

- Mechanical or equipment failure;
- Injury caused by the action or inaction of other passengers or other watercraft
